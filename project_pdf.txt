AI LAWYER PROJECT - PDF FORMAT

KEY PROBLEMS (7 points):
1. Emergency lawyer finding is hard; exorbitant fees charged
2. Govt lawyers collect fees without fighting cases
3. Lawyer availability/schedules not accessible
4. Users unaware of nearby courts
5. Lawyers cannot memorize all laws
6. Finding case studies requires significant effort
7. Negligent govt lawyers cause case losses

KEY SOLUTIONS (7 points):
1. Lawyer profiles with case history, wins/losses, star ratings
2. System detects govt lawyers who collect fees without activity
3. Real-time availability: free slots listed, booked as "occupied"
4. Location access shows courts on map with shortest routes (walk/drive)
5. Indian Constitution with full legal text; search highlights within paragraphs
6. Case-type search surfaces relevant case studies for research
7. Complaint portal for reporting lawyers with evidence submission

YOUR 10 APP FEATURES:
1. User Login Page
2. Lawyer Profiles with qualifications, case history, track records
3. AI Legal Assistant
4. Multilingual Voice Support (all languages)
5. Category-wise Classification (Criminal, Civil, Family, Corporate)
6. Transparent Fee Structure on profiles
7. Case-Type Search with win-rate stats & star ratings
8. AI Call Bot (screens calls, intake, forwards when necessary)
9. Auto-Update Constitutional Library (new amendments added automatically)
10. Complaint/Grievance Portal (evidence submission for govt lawyers)

TECHNICAL: Vanilla ES6 JS, LocalStorage, Web Speech API, CSS Variables, FontAwesome. 16 lawyers, 50+ cases, full Constitution, 22 courts.
