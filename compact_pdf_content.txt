AI LAWYER - KEY POINTS

PROBLEMS:
1. Finding lawyer in emergencies is hard; exorbitant fees charged
2. Government lawyers collect fees without fighting cases
3. Lawyer availability/schedules not accessible
4. Users unaware of nearby courts
5. Lawyers cannot memorize all laws
6. Finding case studies requires significant effort
7. Negligent government lawyers cause case losses

SOLUTIONS:
1. Lawyer profiles with case history, wins/losses, star ratings (best to average)
2. System analyzes govt lawyers to detect fee-collectors without case activity
3. Real-time availability: free slots listed, booked as "occupied"
4. Location access shows courts on map with shortest routes (walk/drive/transit)
5. Indian Constitution section with full legal text; search highlights within paragraphs
6. Case-type search surfaces relevant case studies for research/preparation
7. Complaint portal for reporting lawyers with evidence submission

APP FEATURES:
1. User Login Page
2. Lawyer Profiles with qualifications, case history, track records
3. AI Legal Assistant
4. Multilingual Voice Support (all languages)
5. Category-wise Classification (Criminal, Civil, Family, Corporate)
6. Transparent Fee Structure on profiles
7. Case-Type Search with win-rate stats & star ratings
8. AI Call Bot (screens calls, intake, forwards when necessary)
9. Auto-Update Constitutional Library (new amendments added automatically)
10. Complaint/Grievance Portal (evidence submission support for govt lawyers)
