AI LAWYER - Presentation Content

KEY POINTS (Problems):
1. Finding a lawyer during emergencies is difficult. Even when one is found, they often charge exorbitant fees. Having multiple options would make it more convenient for users.
2. Government lawyers often collect fees without actually fighting the case. This should be detectable/flagged.
3. Knowing lawyers' availability/schedules is problematic. This information should be accessible.
4. Users often don't know about nearby courts. Finding them is difficult.
5. It's not possible for lawyers to remember all laws at all times.
6. Finding relevant case studies requires significant effort.
7. If a user doesn't get help from a government lawyer, they lose the case due to that lawyer's negligence.

SOLUTIONS (Features):
1. The app will contain profiles of all lawyers (from best to average), including their case history (wins/losses) and star ratings based on performance, making it easy to find the right lawyer.
2. The app will feature both government and private lawyer profiles. The system will analyze government lawyers to detect those who collect fees without actively fighting cases.
3. Users can easily view lawyers' real-time availability. Free schedules will be listed, and booked slots will show as "occupied."
4. Since users are often unaware of nearby courts, the app will use location access to display them on a map with the shortest routes—offering separate navigation paths for walking, driving, etc.
5. Lawyers can't memorize all laws. The app will include an Indian Constitution section with full legal text. Searching a specific section will highlight it within the full paragraph.
6. Searching by case type will surface relevant case studies, aiding lawyers in research and preparation.
7. Users can file formal complaints against government lawyers with supporting evidence if the lawyer fails to provide adequate assistance.

APP FEATURES:
1. User Login Page
2. Lawyer Profiles with qualifications, case history, and detailed track records
3. AI Legal Assistant
4. Multilingual Voice Support (all languages)
5. Category-wise Classification (e.g., Criminal, Civil, Family, Corporate lawyers)
6. Transparent Fee Structure visible on lawyer profiles
7. Case-Type Search with lawyer win-rate statistics and star ratings per case category
8. AI Call Bot that screens incoming calls, handles initial intake, and forwards to the lawyer only when necessary
9. Auto-Update Constitutional Library — new amendments/laws are automatically added to the app's Constitution section
10. Complaint/Grievance Portal for reporting lawyers (especially government lawyers) with evidence submission support
